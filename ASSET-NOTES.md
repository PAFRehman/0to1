# Graphics and audio provenance

The moving Point Lookout coastline retains the existing project's scenery. Its original procedural scene was rendered once into seamless native-video loops in `public/scenery`, keeping the sky, ocean/waves, terrain, vegetation and camera motion for all devices. Landscape and portrait MP4/WebM variants select a supported decoder; WebP posters provide the first frame. The original procedural scene source is retained under `lib/coast` for study and attribution.

The single articulated blue penguin Pip, walking/foot-contact rig, screen reactions, envelopes, hearts, feather wings and detailed twin-engine fighter jet are original procedural meshes in `lib/penguin-world-engine.ts`. The body shape was inspired by the user-supplied Pudgy Penguins reference https://x.com/pudgypenguins/status/2068011247027515508. It is not an official downloaded Pudgy model or NFT. The second rig and flower presentation were removed for this student studio. The vector guide fallback and layers favicon are original SVG code.

`public/audio/calm.mp3`, `quiet.mp3` and `love.mp3` are the original project's synthesized score, rendered into loops for smooth playback. `lib/story-music.ts` preserves crossfades, chimes and the jet sound. It does not redistribute a commercial song or the referenced promotional clip.

No generated website screenshots are shipped as project assets. The 3D scenery, posters and actor assets are retained from the provided project; no official NFT artwork is claimed as the student's original design. Preserve upstream notices included in source files.
